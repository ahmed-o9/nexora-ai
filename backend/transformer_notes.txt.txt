A transformer is an electrical device that transfers AC
electrical energy from one circuit to another through
electromagnetic induction.

The primary winding is connected to the AC supply.
Alternating current in the primary winding produces
alternating magnetic flux in the transformer core.

This changing magnetic flux links the secondary winding
and induces an EMF in the secondary winding.

A step-up transformer increases voltage and decreases
current, while a step-down transformer decreases voltage
and increases current.
